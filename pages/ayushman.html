Ayushman Bharat Yojana

📌 Service Introduction

Under the Ayushman Bharat Yojana, eligible families across the country are provided free health insurance coverage of up to 5 lakh rupees per year at government and empaneled private hospitals.

📋 Required Documents

  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card / Ration Card
  • Mobile Number: Mobile number linked with Aadhaar Card
  • Family Details: Samagra ID or name in the eligible list

⏱️ Time & Fee

⏱️ Estimated Time:

1 to 3 working days (After e-KYC verification)

💰 Government/CSC Fee:

Free by Government | Printing fee: ₹10 - ₹30 at CSC

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. How much free treatment is provided under the Ayushman card?

Ans: It provides cashless treatment of up to 5 lakh rupees per family per year.

Q2. Is it mandatory to have a name in the beneficiary list?

Ans: Yes, your name must be present in the SECC database or ration card eligibility list.
